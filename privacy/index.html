Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Still Up (“we,” “us”) operates the Still Up mobile application and this website at stillup.mom. This policy describes what information we handle and how we use it.

Who this covers

People who install the Still Up app or visit stillup.mom.

Information we may collect

  • Account information — if you create an account: email address and a password or sign-in token from the provider you choose (for example Sign in with Apple or Google).
  • App activity you enter — content you save in the app so the product can work.
  • Device and diagnostics — device type, OS version, crash logs, and coarse performance data so we can keep the app stable.
  • Support mail — whatever you send to hello@stillup.mom.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your content to train third-party foundation models.

How we use it

  • Run the app and sync your data across devices if you sign in.
  • Reply to support requests.
  • Fix bugs and prevent abuse.
  • Meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information only with infrastructure providers that host the app or this site (for example Apple, Google, Cloudflare, Vercel) under their terms, or if the law requires it. We do not share data with advertisers.

Retention

Account and app data stay until you delete the account or ask us to delete it. Support email is kept only as long as needed to resolve the request.

Delete your account or data

If the app has an account, use the in-app delete-account control when it is available. You can also email hello@stillup.mom from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm when it is gone.

Children

Still Up is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.
